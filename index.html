A clearer way to check

How Dawa Verify works.

We turn a medicine photo into structured information and transparent safety signals.

01

See

Take photos of the front, back, and the batch or expiry area. The system reads only what is visible on the package.

02

Understand

Qwen Vision extracts the medicine name, strength, manufacturer, batch, expiry, and printed instructions.

03

Verify

Deterministic rules check dates, required fields, and reference catalogue matches separately from the AI reading.

04

Act

Review the evidence, listen in a supported language, or save confirmed stock for pharmacy monitoring.

What it does not do

A photo cannot prove that a medicine is genuine or counterfeit. When something is uncertain, Dawa Verify says so and points you to a pharmacist or official source.
